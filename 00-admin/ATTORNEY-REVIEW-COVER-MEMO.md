## 1. What This Package Is

Flora Summit LLC is entering into a brand-foundation and exclusive sweepstakes-partnership structure with an individual founder/operator and a newly proposed LLC brand owner, in connection with a cannabis-industry client relationship. The attached documents:

- assign brand assets (logos, packaging, word marks) from the individual Founder into a newly formed brand-owner LLC;
- license those assets back to the Founder acting as Authorized Operator;
- grant Flora Summit an exclusive appointment, with a ten (10) year initial term and successive five (5) year auto-renewals, to administer sweepstakes/promotional-drawing activities for this brand.

This is intended to become a **reusable template** for future Flora Summit brand-client relationships, so the goal of this review is not just to clear this specific deal but to establish reviewed baseline language Flora Summit can reuse with future clients without a full re-review each time.
