# OCD Exclusive Sweepstakes Partnership Agreement

**Document ID:** OCD-FS-001
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Draft — Attorney Review Required

This Agreement is entered into as of the date this Agreement is fully executed by all Parties below (the “Effective Date”), by and among:

- **OCD LLC** (“Brand Owner”);
- **Duy Hoang Thai, individually** (“Authorized Operator”);
- **Duy Hoang Thai**, individually, solely for the limited Founder covenant expressly stated herein; and
- **Flora Summit LLC** (“Flora Summit”).

## 1. Partnership Purpose

The Parties desire to establish a coordinated long-term relationship in which Flora Summit serves as the exclusive partner for OCD-branded sweepstakes and related prize-promotion activities.

## 2. OCD Sweepstakes Activities

“OCD Sweepstakes Activities” means sweepstakes, promotional drawings, prize promotions, and substantially similar chance-based promotional programs conducted under, using, featuring, funded through, or materially associated with the OCD Brand or its goodwill.

The term does not include:

- ordinary coupons or fixed discounts;
- bona fide loyalty rewards with no element of chance;
- private employee incentives;
- charitable activities that do not use OCD goodwill commercially; or
- genuinely independent brands not constituting an OCD continuation.

## 3. Exclusive Appointment

Brand Owner appoints Flora Summit as the sole and exclusive commercial partner to develop, administer, operate, coordinate, or authorize OCD Sweepstakes Activities.

Authorized Operator acknowledges and agrees to honor the appointment.

No Party shall knowingly authorize another provider to perform OCD Sweepstakes Activities without Flora Summit's prior written consent.


## 3.1 Shared Program Carveout

Flora Summit may operate Shared Promotional Programs involving other brands, retailers, sponsors, authorized operators, and participants.

OCD's exclusive appointment applies to OCD Sweepstakes Activities and does not grant Brand Owner, Authorized Operator, or Founder exclusivity over the Flora Summit Platform or over unrelated brands or retailers.

OCD may participate in a shared Flora Summit campaign without waiving or reducing Flora Summit's exclusive appointment for OCD Sweepstakes Activities. No other participant acquires ownership or license rights in the OCD Brand except as expressly authorized in writing.

## 4. Term

The exclusive appointment granted under this Agreement is brand-specific and has an initial term of **ten (10) years** from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").

Following the Initial Term, the appointment automatically renews for successive **five (5) year** periods (each, a "Renewal Term") unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

The appointment, once in effect, survives expiration or termination of campaign-level documents, Order Forms, Statements of Work, and ordinary service relationships for the remainder of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

The exclusive appointment granted under this Agreement may be terminated prior to the end of its then-current term only as follows:

(a) by either Party upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail;

(b) by either Party immediately upon written notice if the other Party's conduct becomes illegal or prohibited under Applicable Law in connection with OCD Sweepstakes Activities, including loss of any license required to lawfully perform this Agreement;

(c) by either Party immediately upon written notice in the event of the other Party's fraud or willful misconduct materially related to this Agreement;

(d) by either Party immediately upon the other Party's insolvency, assignment for the benefit of creditors, or filing of a bankruptcy petition (whether voluntary or involuntary, if not dismissed within sixty (60) days); or

(e) by mutual written release executed by all Parties, subject to any limits required for enforceability under Applicable Law.

A campaign pause, lack of an active campaign, or change in service scope does not by itself terminate the appointment.

## 5. Authorized Operator Acknowledgment

Authorized Operator shall not directly or indirectly conduct, commission, outsource, sponsor, fund, license, or authorize OCD Sweepstakes Activities outside Flora Summit's appointment.

The appointment still applies when a processor, distributor, retailer, manufacturer, affiliate, prize supplier, or other participant is named as sponsor, advertiser, supplier, or contracting party.

## 6. Limited Founder Covenant

Duy Hoang Thai personally covenants that he will not knowingly use, direct, fund, cause, or participate in:

- a Controlled Entity;
- nominee;
- intermediary;
- undisclosed side agreement;
- under-the-table payment;
- referral arrangement;
- related operator; or
- another structure

for the principal purpose or material effect of moving OCD Sweepstakes Activities outside Flora Summit's appointment.

This covenant:

- is limited to OCD and its goodwill;
- does not guarantee performance by every separate entity;
- does not make Duy Hoang Thai personally liable for all company obligations;
- does not prohibit disclosed ordinary vendor or retailer payments; and
- does not cover genuinely independent brands.

## 7. Controlled Entities and Related Operators

Brand Owner and Authorized Operator shall not knowingly use a Controlled Entity, affiliate, successor, licensee, sublicensee, processor, distributor, management company, IP holding company, or related party to evade the appointment.

Before a controlled or replacement operator materially participates in OCD Sweepstakes Activities, Brand Owner shall cause it to execute an Operator Joinder where legally and commercially appropriate.

## 8. Change of Operator

A change in processor, distributor, manufacturer, manager, licensee, or operating company does not itself terminate Flora Summit's appointment.

Brand Owner shall provide the replacement operator written notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain an acknowledgment before OCD Sweepstakes Activities begin.

## 9. Brand Evolution

The appointment applies to an OCD continuation, successor, replacement, or material evolution determined from the totality of circumstances, including transferred goodwill, customer migration, public positioning, reused principal assets, or a coordinated relaunch principally intended to avoid the appointment.

Common ownership alone does not make a genuinely separate brand subject to this Agreement.

## 10. Brand Transfer and Change of Control

Before transferring material OCD Brand Assets or control of Brand Owner, Brand Owner shall:

1. disclose Flora Summit's appointment to the proposed acquirer;
2. include it in relevant diligence and transaction schedules;
3. obtain a written assumption where required and legally available;
4. avoid representing that the assets are free of the appointment; and
5. give Flora Summit the contractually required notice.

Nothing purports to bind an unrelated buyer beyond applicable law or valid assumption. A failure by Brand Owner to follow this Section remains a breach.

## 11. Confidential or Concealed Arrangements

No Party shall knowingly conceal compensation, referral fees, side payments, nominee relationships, or indirect contracting structures principally intended to defeat Flora Summit's appointment.

This does not prohibit lawful, disclosed compensation paid in the ordinary course to retailers, vendors, processors, agencies, prize suppliers, or service providers.

## 12. Relationship to Flora Legal OS Agreements

This Agreement supplements the Flora Summit Master Commercial Agreement, applicable Order Forms, Statements of Work, and Sweepstakes Services Addendum.

For OCD Sweepstakes Activities, this Agreement controls over a conflicting general non-exclusivity clause, but only to the extent of the conflict.

Campaign economics, payment obligations, operational duties, and compliance responsibilities remain governed by the applicable Flora Summit commercial documents.

## 13. Compliance

Every OCD Sweepstakes Activity must be separately approved and conducted under applicable rules, disclosures, eligibility requirements, advertising restrictions, and regulatory requirements.

Exclusivity does not authorize unlawful conduct and does not guarantee that any proposed campaign will be approved or launched.

## 14. Remedies

In the event of a breach of this Agreement, the non-breaching Party is entitled to:

(a) recover actual damages proximately caused by the breach;

(b) seek equitable relief, including injunctive relief and specific performance, where legally available and where monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy — including to enforce the exclusivity provisions of Section 3 and the Limited Founder Covenant in Section 6;

(c) recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs if it is the prevailing Party in an action to enforce this Agreement; and

(d) a cure period of thirty (30) days following written notice of a curable breach, except that no cure period applies to a breach of Section 3 (Exclusive Appointment) or Section 6 (Limited Founder Covenant), which may be enforced immediately given the risk of irreparable harm to Flora Summit's exclusive rights.

This Agreement does not establish a liquidated damages amount, penalty, or punitive remedy.

## 15. General Terms

**15.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth in the applicable Exhibits or such other address as a Party designates in writing.

**15.2 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Parties.

**15.3 Assignment; Successors.** No Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Parties, except that Flora Summit may assign this Agreement in connection with a sale of substantially all of its assets or a change of control, and Brand Owner may assign this Agreement subject to Section 10 (Brand Transfer and Change of Control). This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties' permitted successors and assigns.

**15.4 Waiver.** No waiver of any provision of this Agreement is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving Party. No waiver of any breach is deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach.

**15.5 Integration.** This Agreement, together with its Exhibits and the agreements referenced in Section 12 (Relationship to Flora Legal OS Agreements), constitutes the entire agreement among the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, written or oral, regarding that subject matter.

**15.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect, and the invalid provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving its intent.

**15.7 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oklahoma, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal courts located in Oklahoma County, Oklahoma.

**15.8 Dispute Resolution.** The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising out of this Agreement through direct negotiation between authorized representatives within thirty (30) days after written notice of the dispute. If the dispute remains unresolved, either Party may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity in the courts identified in Section 15.7. *(No arbitration provision included — disputes proceed in the courts identified above. This is a default choice, not a counsel-reviewed decision; arbitration can be added later if preferred.)*

**15.9 Electronic Signatures; Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original, and all of which together constitute one instrument. Electronic signatures, including signatures transmitted via DocuSign or a similar service, are valid and binding to the same extent as original signatures.

**15.10 Attorneys' Fees.** The prevailing Party in any action to enforce this Agreement is entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs from the non-prevailing Party.

**15.11 Survival.** Sections regarding Exclusive Appointment, Limited Founder Covenant, Confidentiality, Remedies, and this Section 15 survive termination of this Agreement.

**15.12 Opportunity to Consult Counsel.** Each Party acknowledges that it has had the opportunity to review this Agreement with independent legal counsel of its choosing prior to execution, and that any decision not to do so was made knowingly and voluntarily.
